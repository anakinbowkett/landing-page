Creator Programme Agreement

This is the full agreement between you and Monturalearn for the TikTok Creator Programme. Please read it together with a parent or guardian — you'll both need to sign at the bottom. It should be read alongside the Consent Form.

1. Eligibility

1.1 The Creator confirms they are 16 or over and no longer of compulsory school age (i.e. beyond Year 11/GCSEs — in Sixth Form, College, employment, or equivalent).

1.2 Anyone under 16 is not eligible, and this Agreement is void if the Creator does not meet this requirement.

2. What we're asking you to do

2.1 Post 4–6 TikTok videos per week promoting Montura Learn.

2.2 Follow the weekly content brief we send you — including the hook, angle, and key points to cover. If you've shown you can reliably get people clicking through to the site, we're open to you proposing your own approach instead — talk to us.

2.3 Send a screenshot of each video's performance (views, likes, comments, shares) within 24 hours of posting, so we can see what's working and plan what's next.

2.4 Label any video that forms part of this programme clearly as an ad — e.g. "#ad" or "Paid partnership" — in line with UK advertising rules. This applies to every video posted under this Agreement.

2.5 If asked, provide a TikTok ad-authorisation code for a specific video within 1 day, so we can run it as a Spark Ad with a website button. You keep full ownership of and access to your account; this does not let us post from it.

2.6 In plain terms: this code just gives us permission to boost one specific video as a paid ad. It is optional, it costs you nothing, it does not change your £5 per video, and we will show you exactly how to generate it if asked. Any advertising cost involved is paid by Montura Learn to TikTok directly, not deducted from your pay.

3. What you get

3.1 £5 for every video posted that meets the requirements in Clause 2, paid by bank transfer weekly.

3.2 This is a flat rate per video posted. We do not pay based on views, likes, comments, or shares.

3.3 Your first month of a Montura Learn subscription free, if you join the programme within 3 days of signing.

3.4 Your name featured on the Montura Learn homepage while you're an active Creator.

3.5 This is genuine content-creation/marketing experience, which you're welcome to list on your CV.

4. Review and ending the arrangement

4.1 We review videos and results regularly, generally weekly, and may adjust the brief, pause, or end the arrangement with a Creator based on performance or fit.

4.2 Either party may end this Agreement at any time by giving 7 days' written notice (a message or email is fine).

4.3 We may end this Agreement immediately if the Creator posts anything untrue, harmful, discriminatory, sexually suggestive, or otherwise in breach of TikTok's own rules or the standards in Clause 6, or if the eligibility in Clause 1 turns out not to be met.

4.4 Payment is still due for any video already posted and approved at the time this Agreement ends, whichever party ends it.

5. Use of your content

5.1 You grant Montura Learn a non-exclusive licence to host, display, edit, repost, and promote the videos you post under this programme, including running them as paid ads (e.g. TikTok Spark Ads) using the authorisation code you provide under Clause 2.5.

5.2 This licence lasts for the length of this Agreement and up to 12 months after it ends, for videos already posted while it was active.

5.3 You keep ownership of your own TikTok account and content; this Agreement does not transfer your account or copyright to us, only the licence described above.

6. Standards

6.1 Videos must be honest and not make false or misleading claims about Montura Learn.

6.2 Videos must be your own genuine opinion and delivery — we are not asking you to say anything you don't believe.

6.3 We are genuinely open to new ideas — if you think a different hook, format, or approach would work better than the brief, tell us.

7. Payment and tax

7.1 Payments are made by bank transfer to the account details you provide, generally weekly.

7.2 You (or your Parent/Guardian, given your age) are responsible for declaring and paying any tax due on payments received, if applicable.

8. Confidentiality

8.1 Weekly briefs, strategy, and performance data we share with you are for your use in creating content only, and should not be shared publicly or with other brands.

9. Data protection

9.1 We collect your name, date of birth, contact details, bank details, and the content/screenshots you send us, in order to run this programme and pay you.

9.2 We use this information because it is necessary to carry out this Agreement.

9.3 We keep it for as long as you are an active Creator and for a reasonable period after (generally up to 6 years) for our own records, then delete it.

9.4 You or your Parent/Guardian can ask to see, correct, or ask us to delete this information at any time by contacting monturalearn@gmail.com.

9.5 Monturalearn is the data controller for this information.

10. Parent/Guardian guarantee

10.1 The Parent/Guardian named on the Consent Form confirms they have read and understood this Agreement in full, and explained it to the Creator.

10.2 The Parent/Guardian agrees, as a separate and independent obligation from the Creator's own, to be bound by and guarantee the Creator's performance of this Agreement, including the licence granted under Clause 5 and the standards in Clause 6.

10.3 The Parent/Guardian confirms they are the Creator's parent or legal guardian and have the authority to give this consent and guarantee.

11. General

11.1 This Agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.

11.2 If any part of this Agreement is found unenforceable, the rest continues to apply.

11.3 This Agreement, together with the Consent Form, is the entire agreement between the parties.
