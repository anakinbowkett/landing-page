Creator Programme Consent Form

This confirms that you and a parent or guardian have read, understood, and agree to take part in the Creator Programme. Read it together with the full Agreement — they cover everything involved.

1. About this form

This form confirms that the Creator named below and their parent/guardian have read, understood, and agree to the Creator taking part in the Montura Learn Creator Programme, as fully described in the accompanying Creator Programme Agreement. Please read both documents together — they cover everything involved.

2. Eligibility

The Creator confirms they are aged 16 or over, and are no longer of compulsory school age — i.e. they have finished Year 11/GCSEs and are now in Sixth Form, College, employment, or equivalent. Montura Learn does not accept anyone under 16, or anyone still in Year 11 or below, onto this programme.

3. What the programme involves (summary)

  • Posting 4–6 TikTok videos a week promoting Montura Learn
  • Following the weekly content brief (or, once trusted, proposing your own approach)
  • £5 per video posted — not based on views, likes, comments, or shares
  • Sending a performance screenshot within 24 hours of posting

Full detail of all of the above is set out in the Creator Programme Agreement.

4. About the "Visit Website" button on some videos

  • Sometimes we might turn one of your videos into a TikTok ad, so more people see it, with a button that takes them straight to our website.
  • To do this, TikTok needs a special code from you — it just gives us permission to boost that one video. It does NOT give us your password or access to your account.
  • This is optional — we'll only ask for videos that are already doing well, and you can say no.
  • This costs Montura Learn money, like any TikTok ad. It costs you nothing and does not change your £5 per video.
  • We'll show you exactly how to generate the code — it only takes a minute.

5. What we need from the Parent/Guardian

By signing below, you confirm that:

  • (a) You are the parent or legal guardian of the Creator named below;
  • (b) You have read this Consent Form and the Agreement in full, and are satisfied you understand the payment structure, content requirements, and how the Creator's videos may be used;
  • (c) You have discussed this with the Creator, and you consent to them taking part;
  • (d) You agree, as set out in the Agreement, to be bound by and guarantee the Creator's obligations under it.

6. Data protection

Montura Learn collects the Creator's name, date of birth, contact details, bank details (for payment), video/engagement content, and both signatures on this form, in order to run the programme and pay the Creator. Full detail is in the Agreement's Data Protection clause. Monturalearn is the data controller.

7. Right to withdraw

The Creator or Parent/Guardian may withdraw from the programme at any time by giving written notice, as set out in the Agreement.
